What to Expect

If you are reading this, you probably just went under contract. Congratultions!

Now the fun starts. Your realtor handed you a stack of 3 cards or a list with 3 home inspectors phone numbers on it and said you have 10 days to get a home inspection done. You have no idea who to choose, and you don't know what to expect from a home inspection. You just want to get it over with and move on to the next step in the process.

I get it. I was in your shoes once too. I had no idea what to expect from a home inspection when I bought my first house. Honestly, I didn't know what to look for the second time I bought a house either. My goal as a home inspector is to make sure you know what to expect from a home inspection, and to provide you with a detailed report on the condition of the house. I want you to be able to sit at the closing table with confidence, knowing that you made an informed decision.

Most real estate contracts have a due diligence period of 10 days. During this time, you have the opportunity to get a home inspection done, and to negotiate with the seller if any issues are found. As a home inspector, I will perform a visual inspection of various systems and components of the house and will provide you with a detailed report that includes photos and descriptions of any issues that are found. I will also provide you with recommendations for repairs or further evaluations if necessary. My goal is to help you make an informed decision about the condition of the house, and to give you peace of mind as you move forward with your purchase.
