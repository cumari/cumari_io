About Cumari Inspections

Cumari Inspections was founded on integrity, service, and a commitment to protecting property buyers. As an Air Force veteran and experienced real estate professional, I understand both the technical and financial sides of a property transaction. Buying a home or commercial building is often one of the largest investments an individual or business will ever make — and you deserve clear, honest information before making that decision.

A professional inspection is more than a checklist — it's protection for your investment. Using a trained eye, inspections identify defects, safety hazards, structural concerns, and system issues that many people may overlook during a typical showing. Recognizing these conditions early allows buyers to negotiate from a position of strength, plan for future maintenance, and reduce the risk of costly surprises after closing.

Inspections are performed in accordance with Louisiana Standards of Practice, focusing on the observable condition of the property at the time of the inspection.

My goal is simple: provide thorough, unbiased evaluations so you can move forward with confidence. You'll receive clear explanations, detailed findings, and the peace of mind that comes from understanding the condition of your property.
