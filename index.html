What to Expect

If you are reading this, you probably just went under contract. Congratultions!

Now the fun starts. Your realtor handed you a stack of 3 cards or a list with 3 home inspectors phone numbers on it and said you have 10 days to get a home inspection done. You have no idea who to choose, and you don't know what to expect from a home inspection. You just want to get it over with and move on to the next step in the process.

I get it. I was in your shoes once too. I had no idea what to expect from a home inspection when I bought my first house. Honestly, I didn't know what to look for the second time I bought a house either.

You probably looked at multiple houses before making a decision, but you didn't have a chance to look at every single detail of the house. Some people know what to look at before buying a house, but most people don't. That's where I come in. I will look at the house objectively and give you a detailed report of what I find. I will also explain everything to you in plain English so you can understand what I'm talking about.

The inspection report I provide will gives you leverage in negotiating repairs and will be used by your home warranty company to determine what repairs are covered under your home warranty. I will also be available to answer any questions you have after the inspection is complete. I want you to feel confident in your purchase and I want you to be happy with your new home.
